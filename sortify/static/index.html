Playlists

Mark your inputs (where new songs land) and the homes you sort into. Marked playlists stay pinned on top.

This list is only re-read from Spotify when you press Refresh, so a playlist you created in the Spotify client since then won't be here yet — and sorting or splitting one you edited there works from the old track list. Refresh first in that case.

Created homes have no folder until you re-export the folder tree from the desktop client — they work as homes either way.
